# retail-ai-ml-sap-case-studies
From Store to Strategy - shelf-level visibility to executive decisions.

A portfolio case study based on my experience piloting a retail process that connected sales-team product photos, AI/ML image processing, SAP data, a data lake, and reports for business leaders.
I steered a  project -  a retail execution process connecting sales-team product photos, AI/ML image processing, SAP back-end data, and a data lake to deliver role-based reports from store teams through to the CFO. The pilot recorded a 40% sales increase against an 18% expected POS target.THis won us a global project increasing the revenue of the company immensely .

> This repository is a sanitized case study. Any demo uses synthetic data and is not the former employer’s code, data, or production architecture.

## The business challenge

Retail teams and the entire sales teams hierarchy till the CFO needed better visibility of product availability in stores. Business and regional leaders also needed timely reports to understand store-level execution and make informed decisions.

## How the solution worked

1. Sales teams photographed products in retail stores using the company app.
2. Images were processed through AI/ML services trained to recognise products and identify availability issues.
3. The senior management got an view of how the products/store etc were performing compared to their expectations bridging the gap and helping dp corrective measures hence improving the sale.
4. AI/ML outputs and SAP-related business data were available through the data lake.
5. Reports provided relevant insights to sales teams, regional teams, sales leadership and the CFO.
